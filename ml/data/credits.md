# Dataset credits

| Dataset | Use | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTSinger | golden set (8 singers), new-voice A/B and morph profiles (other 12 singers); never training | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | https://huggingface.co/datasets/AaronZ345/GTSinger (rev `d78cd68d`) |

GTSinger: Zhang et al., "GTSinger: A Global Multi-Technique Singing Corpus with Realistic Music Scores for All Singing Tasks", NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks.
| VocalSet | training (singing, techniques) | CC BY 4.0 | https://zenodo.org/records/1442513 (VocalSet11.zip, v1.1) |
| VCTK 0.92 | training (speech, speaker diversity) | CC BY 4.0 | https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443 |

VocalSet: Wilkins et al., "VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset", ISMIR 2018.
VCTK: Yamagishi, Veaux, MacDonald, "CSTR VCTK Corpus: English Multi-speaker Corpus for CSTR Voice Cloning Toolkit (version 0.92)", University of Edinburgh, 2019.
